# Fonts

All bundled fonts are free under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (from Google Fonts): Cinzel, Playfair Display, Yatra One, Anton, Poppins, Cardo, PT Serif.
On Windows, Buzz also offers Palatino, Georgia, Segoe UI Black and Impact from the PC; on Linux it uses Cardo, PT Serif, Poppins Black and Anton in their place.
